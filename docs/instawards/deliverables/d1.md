# D1 — Instant USDC reward rail

> **Statement of Work, Deliverable 1.** A USDC payout engine with no single point of failure. Every payout leaves a native Stellar 2-of-N multisig account and is co-signed by an independent policy service. That service re-checks the task and enforces a daily cap before it signs. Centient pays the fee in XLM, and a separate multisig cold reserve refills the hot float. Full wording in the [Statement of Work](../statement-of-work.md), §4.1; evidence in §6.1.

**Target week:** 1 (7–13 September) · **Status:** ✅ **Complete and closed.** QA passed 28/28 at [`263be4c`](https://github.com/artisam-centient/centient/commit/263be4cd5ab06103d965044c6a8bd3c40678f308) and recorded the verdict on 14 September. Promoted to `main` via PR #92 on the same day.

## What a contributor gets when this is done

Their reward comes from an account where **no single key, and no single server, can move the money**. Each payment carries two signatures, which anyone can see on stellar.expert. One of them comes from a service that checked the task ledger independently. The contributor holds no XLM and pays no fee, so the full reward arrives.

## Known limits, stated rather than omitted

* **The co-signer's isolation is simulated at the server boundary.** It shares a Railway account, repository, CI and database instance with the app. That meets *server* isolation but not *account* isolation, and it fails closed on mainnet. The sprint is testnet only (D-7). → [ADR-0001](https://github.com/artisam-centient/centient/blob/develop/docs/adr/0001-simulated-cosigner-isolation.md)
* **The structural guard's known gap.** A raw `fetch` to Horizon's `/transactions` endpoint would get past the submit-site pattern. It is still stopped because no payout can be built or platform-signed outside the allowlist.
* **Cap alerts are fire-and-forget.** A failed alert send does not block a payout. `lib/health-monitor.ts` raises the same alert from the ledger as a backstop.
* **Accounts changed during the week.** Five multisig accounts appear in the evidence. [Accounts](../reference/evidence.md#accounts) shows which two are deployed and why the others were retired.
