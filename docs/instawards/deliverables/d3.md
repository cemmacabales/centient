## What already exists to build on

* **Quality guards:** gold tasks, rate limits, spam and bias detection and agreement scoring, in `lib/quality.ts`. They are currently keyed to the account and move to the Stellar address in #36.
* **Payout rail:** the D1 multisig service, co-signer and cap, which QA passed at `263be4c`. #37 reuses them unchanged.
* **Reconciler:** `lib/reconciler.ts` already matches withdrawals to on-chain payments. #40 extends it to one payout per submission.
* **Idempotency groundwork:** D1's payments lane already proves zero double-pays under concurrent load, and an ambiguous submit is resolved only on proof and never refunded.

## Design questions to settle before Monday

* **What happens to existing balances (#39)?** Some email accounts held a balance on 15 September. Options: pay them out once to the claimed wallet, keep a withdrawal-only legacy path, or both. Whichever is chosen must be tested.
* **What "pending" means to the contributor (#37).** Status must not say "paid" before Horizon accepts the transaction. Decide the copy and the states: pending, sent, confirmed.
* **Mobile (D-4).** Decide whether a phone browser without WalletConnect is enough for "mobile-first".
