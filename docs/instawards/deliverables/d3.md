## What a contributor can do now

Connect Freighter, either the browser extension or the phone app. Rank a pair of AI responses and give a reason. If the answer passes the quality guards, the reward is sent to the connected wallet as its own two-signature USDC payment. It shows as *pending* until Horizon accepts it, and nothing waits in an off-chain balance for a withdrawal.

## Decisions made during the week

* **Existing balances (#39).** Decided 21 September ([ADR-0007](https://github.com/artisam-centient/centient/blob/develop/docs/adr/0007-retire-accumulate-then-withdraw.md)): a withdrawal-only legacy path.
  * Nothing accrues any more.
  * A balance from before instant payout can still be withdrawn, with no minimum, and the withdraw control shows only while one exists.
  * Earlier accrued answers were backfilled into each contributor's "Total earned" and answer count, once.
* **What "pending" means (#37).** Submit answers `pending`, which is true: the payout is queued, not yet sent. Earnings refresh once the payout is sent, and nothing says "paid" before Horizon accepts it ([ADR-0005](https://github.com/artisam-centient/centient/blob/develop/docs/adr/0005-submit-enqueues-worker-pays.md)).
* **Mobile (D-4).** Decided 22 September: a phone signs in with the Freighter Mobile app over WalletConnect v2, in scope for this deliverable and verified on iOS in #41.
